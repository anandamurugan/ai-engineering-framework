# Definition of Ready

Work is ready for implementation when every applicable criterion below is satisfied.

## Required criteria

- The problem, desired outcome, and acceptance criteria are explicit.
- Scope and exclusions are defined.
- Architecture and governance decisions are approved or clearly out of scope.
- Dependencies, constraints, affected systems, and required access are known.
- The work has an owner and an identified human decision-maker.
- Risk is classified using the [risk classification standard](risk-classification.md).
- Required human checkpoints are identified using the [human-in-the-loop standard](human-in-the-loop.md).
- Validation expectations and completion evidence are defined.
- Open questions do not materially alter the implementation approach.

If a criterion is unmet, the agent must resolve it through available evidence or pause for human direction. The [analyze and plan workflow](../workflows/01-analyze-plan.md) produces the readiness record.
