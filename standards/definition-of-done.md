# Definition of Done

Work is done when every applicable criterion below is satisfied and supported by evidence.

## Required criteria

- Approved acceptance criteria are met.
- The implementation remains within scope and follows repository instructions.
- Tests and proportionate quality checks pass.
- Security, privacy, compliance, accessibility, and operational impacts are addressed.
- Documentation and examples reflect observable behavior.
- Required human reviews and approvals are recorded.
- Changes are traceable to the approved requirement and review record.
- No known critical defect remains; other residual risks are documented and accepted by the appropriate human.
- Deployment, rollback, migration, and monitoring considerations are addressed when applicable.
- Follow-up work is captured without being represented as complete.

Completion claims must identify the validation performed and any criterion that does not apply. Risk-based evidence expectations are defined in the [risk classification standard](risk-classification.md).
