# STORY-STD-INC-001 – Incident Management Standard

## Business Value

Enable coordinated response, trustworthy communications, verified recovery, learning, and accountable closure.

## Problem Statement

Incident severity, actions, communications, evidence, and closure require controlled roles and cannot be delegated to an AI agent.

## Scope

Lifecycle, severity, roles, communications, evidence, recovery, post-incident learning, metrics, AI assistance, and human authority.

## Out of Scope

Organization-specific severity thresholds, staffing schedules, communication channels, and automated remediation.

## Requirements

- Create schema-compliant `STD-INC-001` with enforceable response and evidence controls.
- Require authorized humans for severity changes, destructive remediation, external communications, and closure.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Acceptance Criteria

Lifecycle, severity, roles, communication, learning, metrics, evidence, AI restrictions, and human authority are complete.

## Evidence Expectations

The review package includes metadata, incident evidence definitions, relationship review, and validation results.

## Validation Requirements

Validate schema constraints, sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog entry, and placeholder absence.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, template, target path, response roles, evidence, and decision authorities are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for operations, security, documentation, and Product Owner review.

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
