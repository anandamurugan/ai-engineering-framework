# STD-INF-005 – Standards Catalog

## Business Value

Provide one authoritative navigation and status index for enterprise standards.

## Scope

Document organization, statuses, categories, authoring resources, current entries, and clearly separated planned standards.

## Requirements

- Preserve existing standard documents.
- Avoid links to nonexistent planned standards.
- Prevent duplicate catalog IDs.

## Required Deliverable

[Standards Catalog](../../standards/README.md)

## Dependencies

- [Framework Asset Taxonomy](../../docs/framework/FRAMEWORK_ASSETS.md)
- [Standard Authoring Guide](../../docs/standards/STANDARD_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Required navigation links and catalog columns are present.
- Existing foundational standards are cataloged without inventing metadata.

## Validation Requirements

Validate links, table structure, IDs, and planned/current separation.

## Definition of Ready

Catalog columns, statuses, categories, and source documents are known.

## Definition of Done

The catalog is accurate, link-valid, and ready for Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before this story is complete.
