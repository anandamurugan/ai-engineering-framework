# STORY-STD-REL-001 – Release Management Standard

## Business Value

Provide accountable readiness, go/no-go, communication, execution, verification, and closure for releases.

## Problem Statement

Release decisions require consistent evidence and separation between implementation assistance and human approval.

## Scope

Planning, versioning, readiness, decision controls, execution, hypercare, closure, evidence, metrics, AI boundaries, and human authority.

## Out of Scope

Framework release tagging, vendor-specific release systems, deployment implementation, and Product Owner approval of REL-004.

## Requirements

- Create schema-compliant `STD-REL-001` with testable release controls.
- Retain human go/no-go and release approval authority.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Required Deliverable

[STD-REL-001](../../standards/release/STD-REL-001-release-management-standard.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

Planning, readiness, decision, execution, closure, metrics, evidence, AI restrictions, and approval controls are complete.

## Evidence Expectations

The review package includes metadata, release evidence definitions, relationship review, and validation results.

## Validation Requirements

Validate schema constraints, sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog entry, and prohibited placeholders.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, template, target path, review roles, evidence expectations, and approval authority are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for release, operations, documentation, security, and Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
