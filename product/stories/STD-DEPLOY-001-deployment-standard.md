# STORY-STD-DEPLOY-001 – Deployment Standard

## Business Value

Make environment changes repeatable, traceable, verifiable, recoverable, and subject to authorized production approval.

## Problem Statement

Uncontrolled artifacts, configuration, infrastructure, migrations, promotions, and recovery decisions create material operational risk.

## Scope

Delivery pipelines, infrastructure, configuration, promotion, strategies, data changes, verification, recovery, evidence, metrics, and human gates.

## Out of Scope

Vendor-specific pipelines, deployment products, environment provisioning implementations, and release approval.

## Requirements

- Create schema-compliant `STD-DEPLOY-001` with enforceable deployment and recovery controls.
- Require separate human authority for production deployment and exceptions.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Required Deliverable

[STD-DEPLOY-001](../../standards/deployment/STD-DEPLOY-001-deployment-standard.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

Pipeline, promotion, migration, verification, recovery, metrics, evidence, AI limits, and human gates are complete.

## Evidence Expectations

The review package includes metadata, control-to-evidence mapping, relationship review, and validation results.

## Validation Requirements

Validate schema constraints, required sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog entry, and placeholder absence.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, template, target path, operational risks, evidence, and approval authority are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for architecture, operations, security, documentation, and Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
