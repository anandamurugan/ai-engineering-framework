# STD-INF-007 – Standards Review Process

## Business Value

Ensure standards are correct, enforceable, reviewed by accountable humans, and governed throughout their lifecycle.

## Scope

Define lifecycle stages, roles, criteria, approvals, exceptions, emergency changes, review cycles, versions, deprecation, and evidence.

## Requirements

- Preserve Product Owner final approval.
- Require specialist review when applicable.
- Distinguish minor, major, emergency, deprecated, and retired changes.

## Required Deliverable

[Standards Review Process](../../docs/governance/STANDARD_REVIEW_PROCESS.md)

## Dependencies

- [Human-in-the-Loop Standard](../../standards/human-in-the-loop.md)
- [Governance Model](../../docs/architecture/GOVERNANCE_MODEL.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every required role, review criterion, lifecycle state, and record is defined.
- Agents cannot approve standards or exceptions.

## Validation Requirements

Review lifecycle completeness, authority boundaries, evidence, and links.

## Definition of Ready

Governance sources, statuses, and accountable roles are known.

## Definition of Done

The process passes available checks and is ready for Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before this story is complete.
