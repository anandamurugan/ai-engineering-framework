# STORY-STD-PERF-001 – Performance and Scalability Standard

## Business Value

Make system capacity, responsiveness, efficiency, and growth risks measurable before and after release.

## Problem Statement

Teams need consistent performance objectives, workload models, test evidence, production thresholds, and accountable trade-off decisions.

## Scope

Objectives, budgets, capacity, workload modeling, performance testing, scalable engineering, production monitoring, evidence, metrics, and AI boundaries.

## Out of Scope

Technology-specific tuning, fixed enterprise-wide thresholds, vendor products, and benchmark tooling.

## Requirements

- Create schema-compliant `STD-PERF-001` with reviewable performance and scalability controls.
- Define measurable targets without prescribing one universal threshold.
