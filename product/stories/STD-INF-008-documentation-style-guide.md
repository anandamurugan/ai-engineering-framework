# STD-INF-008 – Framework Documentation Style Guide

## Business Value

Make framework documentation consistent, accessible, maintainable, and reliable for human and AI consumers.

## Scope

Define writing, Markdown, naming, metadata, links, diagrams, examples, terminology, accessibility, and AI-review conventions.

## Requirements

- Define all requested preferred terms.
- Require review of AI-generated content and unsupported claims.
- Keep formal framework content concise and accessible.

## Required Deliverable

[Documentation Style Guide](../../docs/contributing/DOCUMENTATION_STYLE_GUIDE.md)

## Dependencies

- [Framework Asset Taxonomy](../../docs/framework/FRAMEWORK_ASSETS.md)
- [Standard Authoring Guide](../../docs/standards/STANDARD_AUTHORING_GUIDE.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every required style topic and preferred term is addressed.
- Filename, identifier, metadata, and revision-history conventions align with foundation artifacts.

## Validation Requirements

Review heading structure, terminology, links, accessibility guidance, and formatting.

## Definition of Ready

## Definition of Done
