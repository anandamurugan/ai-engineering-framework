# STD-INF-006 – Cross-Reference Model

## Business Value

Make framework relationships explicit, navigable, and suitable for future validation without depending on file locations.

## Scope

Define supported relationship types, direction, requirements, metadata representation, Markdown links, and integrity rules.

## Requirements

- Cover every required relationship and example.
- Distinguish existing links from planned references.
- Prevent broken, circular, and contradictory relationships.

## Required Deliverable

[Cross-Reference Model](../../docs/architecture/CROSS_REFERENCE_MODEL.md)

## Dependencies

- [Framework Asset Taxonomy](../../docs/framework/FRAMEWORK_ASSETS.md)
- [Standard Metadata Schema](../../schemas/standard.schema.yaml)

## Acceptance Criteria

- Relationship semantics and direction are unambiguous.
- Examples connect all required asset combinations.

## Validation Requirements

Review identifier use, relationship consistency, future-reference rules, and links.

## Definition of Ready

Asset types and stable identifier rules are defined.

## Definition of Done

The model passes available checks and is ready for Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before this story is complete.
