# STORY-STD-SEC-001 – Enterprise Security Standard

## Business Value

Reduce enterprise security, privacy, identity, application, supply-chain, and operational risk through reviewable controls.

## Problem Statement

Security obligations require one vendor-neutral contract spanning design, engineering, delivery, and operation while preserving accountable human authority.

## Scope

Security principles, identity, access, secrets, cryptography, application security, secure engineering, supply chain, operations, evidence, metrics, and AI boundaries.

## Out of Scope

Organization-specific policy, product configuration, technology profiles, and automated enforcement tooling.

## Requirements

- Define testable, vendor-neutral mandatory controls.
- Relate security requirements to applicable standards without duplicating authoritative guidance.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Required Deliverable

Draft Enterprise Security Standard at `standards/security/STD-SEC-001-enterprise-security-standard.md`.

## Acceptance Criteria

The planned standard covers security domains, operational controls, metrics, evidence, AI restrictions, and human approval gates using vendor-neutral requirements.

## Validation Requirements

Validate metadata schema constraints, template sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog readiness, and prohibited placeholders when the standard is implemented.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, schema, template, target path, validation expectations, and Product Owner decision-maker are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for required security, architecture, documentation, and Product Owner reviews.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
