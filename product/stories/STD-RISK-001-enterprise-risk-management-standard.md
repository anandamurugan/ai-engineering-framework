# STORY-STD-RISK-001 – Enterprise Risk Management Standard

## Business Value

Make enterprise engineering risks visible, consistently assessed, owned, treated, reviewed, and accepted only by authorized humans.

## Problem Statement

Technical and AI-assisted delivery needs one auditable risk contract spanning identification through closure without allowing automated risk acceptance.

## Scope

Risk domains, identification, assessment, treatment, governance, metrics, evidence, AI assistance, and human acceptance authority.

## Out of Scope

Organization-specific scoring scales, financial models, regulatory interpretations, and automated risk engines.

## Requirements

- Define testable risk-governance controls.
- Make risk ownership, acceptance, escalation, expiry, and closure explicitly human.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Required Deliverable

Draft Enterprise Risk Management Standard at `standards/risk/STD-RISK-001-enterprise-risk-management-standard.md`.

## Acceptance Criteria

The planned standard covers risk domains, identification, assessment, treatment, governance, metrics, evidence, AI restrictions, and human authority.

## Validation Requirements

Validate metadata schema constraints, template sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog readiness, and placeholder absence when the standard is implemented.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, template, target path, evidence expectations, accountable roles, and approval authority are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for risk, architecture, security, documentation, and Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
