# STORY-EFF-GOV-001 – Governance Review, Deferred Findings & Release Closeout

## Business Value

Demonstrate that v0.5 efficiency outcomes are complete, safe, vendor-neutral, traceable, and separately approved by accountable humans.

## Problem Statement

Automated efficiency and validation evidence cannot authorize release, accept residual risk, or prove that deferred findings and governance boundaries received human review.

## Scope

Complete coverage assessment, repository security guidance, ownership visibility, included-finding disposition, documentation, review evidence, release readiness, and controlled closeout preparation.

## Out of Scope

Fabricated approval, automatic release or tagging, deferred secret scanning or branch administration, and v0.6 implementation.

## Requirements

- Architecture, Domain, Documentation, Security, and Product Owner decisions MUST remain distinct human records.
- Automated PASS, AI recommendations, checkpoints, and routing outcomes MUST NOT imply approval or risk acceptance.
- Included v0.4 findings MUST have attributable evidence; deferred and administrative items MUST remain visible.
- Repository security guidance MUST be added without inventing disclosure contacts.
- Release evidence MUST show validation, tests, metrics, known limitations, and vendor-neutrality review.

## Dependencies

[SPR-005-005](../sprints/SPR-005-005-governance-release-closeout.md), [REL-005](../releases/REL-v0.5.md), and all preceding EPIC-002 stories.

## Required Deliverable

A governance review package, finding-disposition record, release-readiness checklist, accurate product tracking, and controlled closeout evidence.

## Acceptance Criteria

- DOM-REL004-001 and SEC-REL004-003 have evidence-backed completion or explicit governed disposition.
- Included and deferred findings remain independently traceable.
- Validation and efficiency evidence cannot be confused with approval.
- Release readiness accurately distinguishes implemented, deferred, administrative, and future work.

## Validation Requirements

Run full repository validation and tests; review traceability, lifecycle, documentation, security, privacy, vendor neutrality, evidence integrity, and human authority.

## Definition of Ready

All implementation stories are reviewable, finding evidence exists, required review roles are known, and release criteria are current.

## Definition of Done

All mandatory reviews and Product Owner approval are recorded without fabrication, included findings are dispositioned, validation and tests pass, and REL-005 is ready for a separate controlled release action.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before this story is complete.

## Implementation Review Evidence

Implementation is complete and **In Review**. The [REL-005 release-readiness package](../../docs/reviews/REL-005-release-readiness.md) contains the end-to-end scenario, invariant coverage, efficiency and performance measurements, vendor-neutrality and security/privacy assessments, v0.4 finding dispositions, remaining-gap dispositions, acceptance reviews, and release checklist.

Architecture, Domain, Documentation, Security, and Product Owner approvals remain Pending. Automated PASS evidence, checkpoints, routing decisions, and implementation completion do not satisfy those human gates.
