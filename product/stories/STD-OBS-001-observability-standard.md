# STORY-STD-OBS-001 – Observability Standard

## Business Value

Enable reliable detection, diagnosis, operational decisions, and service-level management using trustworthy signals.

## Problem Statement

Inconsistent logs, metrics, traces, alerts, health checks, and runbooks impede operations and can expose sensitive information.

## Scope

Telemetry signals, health, service objectives, alerting, dashboards, runbooks, evidence, metrics, AI assistance, and human controls.

## Out of Scope

Vendor-specific telemetry stacks, on-call staffing models, and automated remediation engines.

## Requirements

- Create schema-compliant `STD-OBS-001` with testable observability and readiness controls.
- Protect evidence and sensitive data while defining safe AI-assisted analysis.

## Dependencies

[Sprint SPR-004-003](../sprints/SPR-004-003-enterprise-engineering-standards.md), [Sprint SPR-004-002](../sprints/SPR-004-002-core-engineering-standards.md), and the [Standards Foundation](../sprints/SPR-004-001B-standards-foundation.md).

## Required Deliverable

[STD-OBS-001](../../standards/observability/STD-OBS-001-observability-standard.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

Core signals, reliability controls, readiness, metrics, evidence, AI restrictions, and human authority are complete.

## Evidence Expectations

The review package includes metadata, signal and readiness evidence definitions, cross-reference review, and validation results.

## Validation Requirements

Validate schema constraints, template sections, normative language, links, relationships, catalog entry, and placeholder absence.

## Definition of Ready

The ID, scope, dependencies, schema, template, target path, evidence expectations, and reviewers are known.

## Definition of Done

Acceptance and validation criteria pass and the Draft is ready for operations, security, documentation, and Product Owner review.

## Product Owner Approval

Product Owner approval is required before completion.
