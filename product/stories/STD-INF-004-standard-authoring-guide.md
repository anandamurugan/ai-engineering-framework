# STD-INF-004 – Standard Authoring Guide

## Business Value

Help authors produce concise, testable, non-duplicative standards with clear evidence and accountability.

## Scope

Explain standard design, metadata, normative rules, evidence, validation, AI guidance, human review, exceptions, relationships, and lifecycle.

## Requirements

- Distinguish standards from adjacent asset types.
- Include strong and weak mandatory-rule examples.
- Define vendor-neutral authoring and review expectations.
